Find the craft beer you'll love, and where to buy it

MyBeerFinder is a free tool that helps you discover craft beer by what it actually tastes like, then shows you where to buy it cheapest across the UK's biggest supermarkets, Tesco, Morrisons, Sainsbury's and Asda. Search by hop, brewery, beer name or flavour, and every beer we list comes decoded.

The idea is simple. Craft beer cans are covered in names most people don't recognise, Citra, Mosaic, Neck Oil, NEIPA, and it's hard to know what you're buying until you've opened it. We translate all of that into plain language.
