About the brewery

Hopvale Brewing Co. is a small independent brewery tucked into a converted dairy on the edge of Bristol. Since 2019 we've been chasing one thing: bright, modern beer with the hops turned up and nothing to hide behind. Everything is brewed in small batches, unfined and unfiltered, so what lands in the glass is exactly what came out of the tank.

We're a team of four who care a bit too much about water chemistry and dry-hop timing. You'll find our cans in independent bottle shops across the South West, poured at our Thursday-to-Sunday taproom, and — now — right here on MyBeerFinder, where drinkers can see exactly what each beer tastes like before they buy.
