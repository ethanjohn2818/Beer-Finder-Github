Privacy policy 🔒

Last updated August 2026.

MyBeerFinder is a simple site that helps you find craft beers and where to buy them. We keep this short and plain.

What we collect

You can use the whole site without an account, the beer search runs in your browser and we don't collect your name or contact details unless you email us.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and a username you choose, plus the beers you mark as "had", so we can log you in, remember your list, give you recommendations and show you on the leaderboard. Only your username and beer count are shown publicly on the leaderboard, never your email. Accounts and login are handled by Supabase. We may occasionally email account holders with updates (e.g. new beers), you can opt out any time. To delete your account and data, email hello@mybeerfinder.co.uk.

Cookies & advertising

We show adverts through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

Because we show ads to visitors in the UK and Europe, we use Google's certified consent tool. The first time you visit from one of those regions it asks whether you're happy with advertising cookies, and only sets them if you agree. You can change or withdraw your choice any time using the button below.

We also store two small preferences in your browser so the site works: your light/dark theme choice and that you've confirmed you're 18 or over. These stay on your device, aren't used to track you, and don't require consent.

Prices & availability

Beer prices, images and links are gathered from supermarket websites and may be out of date. Always check the retailer's own page before buying. We are not affiliated with any supermarket.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hello@mybeerfinder.co.uk.
