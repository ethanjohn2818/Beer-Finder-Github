About Beer Finder 🍺

What this site is, and how to find the perfect beer for you.

What is Beer Finder?

Beer Finder helps you discover craft beer and see exactly where to buy it at UK supermarkets. We scan leading supermarkets for real craft beers, then tell you what each one tastes like — right down to the hops that give it its flavour — and where it's cheapest.

No jargon, no guesswork. If you've ever stood in the beer aisle wondering what something actually tastes like, this is for you.

How to use it

  1. Search — on the page, type a beer, brewery or hop (try "Citra" or "BrewDog"). Or hit Show all beers to browse everything.
  2. Filter & sort — narrow by beer type or supermarket, and sort by price or ABV to find your match.
  3. Not sure what you like? — use and tap the flavours you enjoy (fruity, hoppy, dark…) — we'll show beers that fit.
  4. Tap any beer — to open its page and see its full taste profile, the hops it uses and what each one brings, and where to buy it.
  5. Explore — browse by or by to learn as you go.

A few things to know

Prices, images and links come from the supermarkets' own websites and can change — always double-check on the retailer's page before buying. We're not affiliated with any supermarket. Please drink responsibly. 🍻

Privacy policy 🔒

Last updated August 2026.

Beer Finder is a simple site that helps you find craft beers and where to buy them. We keep this short and plain.

What we collect

You can use the whole site without an account — the beer search runs in your browser and we don't collect your name or contact details unless you email us.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and a username you choose, plus the beers you mark as "had", so we can log you in, remember your list, give you recommendations and show you on the leaderboard. Only your username and beer count are shown publicly — never your email. Accounts and login are handled by Supabase. To delete your account and data, email hello@mybeerfinder.co.uk.

Cookies & advertising

We show adverts through Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to show and measure ads, including personalised ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and opt out of personalised advertising at google.com/settings/ads.

Because we show ads to visitors in the UK and Europe, we use Google's certified consent tool. The first time you visit from one of those regions it asks whether you're happy with advertising cookies, and only sets them if you agree. You can change or withdraw your choice any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

We also store two small preferences in your browser so the site works — your light/dark theme choice and that you've confirmed you're 18 or over. These stay on your device, aren't used to track you, and don't require consent.

Prices & availability

Beer prices, images and links are gathered from supermarket websites and may be out of date. Always check the retailer's own page before buying. We are not affiliated with any supermarket.
