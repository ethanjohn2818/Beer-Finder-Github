Most of us buy beer by grabbing a familiar label and hoping for the best. What are hops, and why do they matter?

Hops are the green flowers of the hop plant, and they're what give beer both its bitterness and, in modern craft beer especially, its aroma and flavour. Added early in the boil they balance the sweetness of the malt; added late or after fermentation (a technique called dry-hopping) they load a beer with fruity, citrussy, piney or tropical character without adding much bitterness. That second job is the reason a hazy IPA can taste like a fruit smoothie.

Each hop variety has its own signature. Citra and Mosaic lean tropical and citrussy; Galaxy bursts with passionfruit; Simcoe and Chinook bring pine and resin; classic English hops like Fuggles and East Kent Goldings are earthy, herbal and gently floral. Once you learn which hops you enjoy, you can recognise them across dozens of different beers.

How to use this page

Every hop listed here links to the supermarket beers we've found that use it, so you can go straight from a flavour you like to a beer you can buy tonight. Search by hop name, or by a flavour, try "citrus", "pine" or "tropical", to see which hops deliver it. Want to read more? Breweries we know 🏭

The craft breweries on your supermarket shelf

Craft beer isn't just a taproom thing any more, the big supermarkets now stock beers from dozens of independent breweries, from household names like BrewDog, Beavertown and Thornbridge to smaller regional favourites. This page brings them together in one place: browse by brewery, see where each one is based, and jump straight to the beers of theirs we've found across Tesco, Morrisons, Sainsbury's and Asda.

Knowing the brewery is a genuinely useful shortcut. Breweries tend to have a house style, one might specialise in soft, juicy hazy IPAs, another in crisp lagers or rich stouts, so once you've found a brewer whose beers you like, exploring the rest of their range is a reliable way to find your next favourite. Search by name if you know who you're after, or by town or city to discover who's brewing near you.

We also flag supermarket own-brand ranges, like Asda Exclusive, which are brewed by third parties and sold under the shop's name. New beers appear here automatically each time we refresh our listings, so the line-up keeps growing.

About MyBeerFinder 🍺

What this site is, and how to find the perfect beer for you.

What is MyBeerFinder?

MyBeerFinder helps you discover craft beer and see exactly where to buy it at UK supermarkets. We scan leading supermarkets for real craft beers, then tell you what each one tastes like, right down to the hops that give it its flavour, and where it's cheapest.

No jargon, no guesswork. If you've ever stood in the beer aisle wondering what something actually tastes like, this is for you.

How to use it

  1. Search, on the page, type a beer, brewery or hop (try "Citra" or "BrewDog"). Or hit Show all beers to browse everything.
  2. Filter & sort, narrow by beer type or supermarket, and sort by price or ABV to find your match.
  3. Not sure what you like?, use and tap the flavours you enjoy (fruity, hoppy, dark…), we'll show beers that fit.
  4. Tap any beer, to open its page and see its full taste profile, the hops it uses and what each one brings, and where to buy it.
  5. Explore, browse by or by to learn as you go.

A few things to know

Prices, images and links come from the supermarkets' own websites and can change, always double-check on the retailer's page before buying. We're not affiliated with any supermarket. Please drink responsibly. 🍻

Last updated August 2026.

MyBeerFinder is a simple site that helps you find craft beers and where to buy them. We keep this short and plain.

What we collect

You can use the whole site without an account, the beer search runs in your browser and we don't collect your name or contact details unless you email us.

Accounts (optional)

If you create an account, we store your email address and a username you choose, plus the beers you mark as "had", so we can log you in, remember your list, give you recommendations and show you on the leaderboard. Only your username and beer count are shown publicly, never your email. Accounts and login are handled by Supabase. To delete your account and data, email hello@mybeerfinder.co.uk.

Because we show ads to visitors in the UK and Europe, we use Google's certified consent tool. The first time you visit from one of those regions it asks whether you're happy with advertising cookies, and only sets them if you agree. You can change or withdraw your choice any time using the Cookie settings link in the footer.

We also store two small preferences in your browser so the site works: your light/dark theme choice and that you've confirmed you're 18 or over. These stay on your device, aren't used to track you, and don't require consent.
