Idaho 7 hops: tropical fruit with a piney twist

Published 14 August 2026 · by Katie

Idaho 7 is one of the newer American hops to earn a permanent place in brewers' recipes, and it's easy to see why: it bridges the gap between the old-school piney hops and the modern tropical ones, offering a bit of both in a single variety.

What Idaho 7 tastes like

Expect tropical fruit and citrus, apricot, mango and orange, alongside a distinctive pine and black tea character that gives it real complexity. It's fruity enough for a hazy IPA but resinous enough for a West Coast one, which is why it's turned up in both.

Where it comes from

Grown in Idaho in the US, Idaho 7 was released in the mid-2010s and quickly became a brewer favourite for its high oil content and versatile flavour. It's a big, punchy hop that delivers a lot of aroma per gram.

How it's used

Idaho 7 works beautifully alongside Citra and Mosaic, adding a piney, tea-like backbone to their fruitiness. It appears in many modern double IPAs and West Coast revivals where its dual character can do double duty. Thornbridge's West Side Glory is one gold-medal example that leans on it.
