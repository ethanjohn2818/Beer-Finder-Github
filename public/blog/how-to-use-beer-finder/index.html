How to use MyBeerFinder: a beginner's guide

Published 22 August 2026 · by Katie

Standing in the supermarket beer aisle, faced with a wall of colourful cans covered in words like "hazy", "Citra" and "double dry-hopped", is genuinely confusing — even for people who love beer. MyBeerFinder exists to take that guesswork away: it shows you what each craft beer actually tastes like and where to buy it cheapest.

If you're brand new to craft beer, here's how to use the site, walked through in the order you'll actually come across each feature.

1. The "are you 18?" pop-up

The very first thing you'll see is a quick age check — beer is an adult product, so we ask you to confirm you're 18 or over. Tick it once and it's remembered on your device, so you won't be asked again.

2. Search — the heart of the site

Straight away you'll land on the search box. You can type three kinds of thing:

  • A beer name — like "Neck Oil" or "Punk IPA", if you already have one in mind.
  • A brewery — like "BrewDog" or "Beavertown", to see everything of theirs we stock.
  • A hop — like "Citra" or "Mosaic", if you've enjoyed a beer with that flavour before.

New to all this and don't know where to start? Under the search box are three quick buttons. Tap Show all beers to browse everything at once, or Alcohol-free if you're after a 0.0% or low-alcohol option. There's a Clear button to reset whenever you like.

3. Sort and filter your results

Once beers appear, a toolbar lets you tidy the list to suit you:

  • Sort by relevance, brewery A–Z, price (low to high is handy on a budget) or strength (ABV).
  • Type — narrow to a style you fancy, like IPA, lager or pale ale.
  • Stores — show only the supermarkets you actually shop at.

A little counter tells you how many beers match, so you always know how much you're looking at.

4. Reading a beer card

Each result is a card with everything you need at a glance. The clever bit is the pricing: a beer is often sold at more than one shop and in more than one pack size, so each card lets you flip between shops and pack sizes, and we highlight the cheapest option for you. No mental maths required.

5. Compare beers side by side

Can't decide between a few? Tick the Compare box on up to four beers and open the comparison. You'll see them lined up together with the cheapest price for the size you want, the hops in each, and a tidy list of the flavours to expect — with any repeated flavours shown only once. It's the fastest way to pick a winner.

6. Tap a beer to see its full story

Click any beer to open its own page. This is where craft beer stops being a mystery: you get the full taste profile, the hops it's brewed with and what each hop actually brings (citrus, tropical fruit, pine and so on), plus every shop that sells it and the price. If you're learning, spend a minute here — it's the quickest way to start connecting names to flavours.

7. Not sure what you like? Find by flavour

This is the feature we'd point every newcomer to. Open Find Your Flavour and simply tap the flavours that sound good to you — fruity, hoppy, dark, citrus, tropical — and we'll show the beers that match. You don't need to know a single brand or hop name; just pick what sounds tasty and let the site do the rest.

8. Learn as you go: Hops and Breweries

When you're ready to go a little deeper, two pages help you learn:

  • Hops — every hop found in the beers you can buy right now. Tap one to see its flavours and which beers use it. Great for chasing a taste you've enjoyed.
  • Brewery — browse the breweries we stock, sorted however you like, and jump straight to their beers. Handy once you've found a brewery whose style suits you.

9. Make an account and track what you've tried

Create a free account — no password needed, just sign in with a link sent to your email, or with Google. Once you're in, every beer has an "I've had this" button. Tick the beers you've tried and two nice things happen:

  • You get personal recommendations, based on the hops and flavours of the beers you've enjoyed.
  • You build up a tally of everything you've tasted — a little diary of your craft-beer journey.

Pick a username when you sign up; it's the only thing shown publicly (never your email).

10. The Leaderboard

Feeling competitive? The Leaderboard has two charts: the most-loved beers (the ones ticked "had" by the most people — a great shortlist if you're stuck), and the top beer hunters (who's tried the most). Tick more beers to climb the ranks.

11. Gifts and merch

Finally, the Gifts page has glassware, gift sets and beer-lover merch — a good shout if you're buying for someone who enjoys a proper pint.

A quick note on prices

We gather prices, pictures and links straight from the supermarkets' own websites, and they can change. Always give the retailer's page a final check before you buy. And of course — please enjoy craft beer responsibly. 🍻

The short version

If you remember nothing else: search for anything you like the sound of, or use Find Your Flavour to pick by flavour, tap a beer to learn what's in it, and make an account to get recommendations as you go. That's craft beer, demystified.
