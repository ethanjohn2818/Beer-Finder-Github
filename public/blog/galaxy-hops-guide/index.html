Galaxy hops: the passionfruit bomb from Australia

Published 19 August 2026 · by Katie

Some hops whisper; Galaxy shouts. This Australian variety is one of the most intensely tropical hops available, and a little goes a very long way. If you've had a beer that tasted like liquid passionfruit, there's a good chance Galaxy was behind it.

What Galaxy tastes like

Galaxy is all about passionfruit and peach, backed by citrus and a hint of that fresh-cut-grass greenness. It's juicy, loud and unmistakably tropical, closer to a fruit cocktail than the pine-and-grapefruit of older hops. Because its aroma is so concentrated, brewers often use it as the star of a beer rather than a supporting player.

Where it comes from

Galaxy was bred in Australia and released in the early 2000s. It has one of the highest total oil contents of any hop, which is exactly why its aroma hits so hard. It thrives in hazy IPAs and juicy pale ales, where its fruitiness gets to take centre stage without being scrubbed away by heavy bitterness.

How it's used

You'll most often find Galaxy dry-hopped, added after the boil, to keep all that passionfruit aroma intact. It pairs beautifully with Citra and Mosaic, and turns up in countless modern hazy IPAs. On its own, as a single-hop beer, it's an unforgettable introduction to just how fruity a hop can be.

Drink it fresh

Like all big aroma hops, Galaxy fades. Its brightest passionfruit notes are at their best within a couple of months of canning, so check the date and drink it young.
