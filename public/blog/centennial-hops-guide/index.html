Centennial hops: the 'super Cascade'

Published 13 August 2026 · by Katie

Nicknamed the "super Cascade", Centennial takes everything people love about that classic American hop and turns up the volume. It's a cornerstone of American IPAs and one of the most dependable citrus hops a brewer can reach for.

What Centennial tastes like

Centennial delivers bold citrus, lemon and grapefruit, with a floral lift and a clean, firm bitterness. It's brighter and more intense than Cascade, but shares that same crisp, refreshing character. It's the sort of hop that makes an IPA taste unmistakably "American".

Where it comes from

Released in the US in 1990, Centennial became a defining hop of the American IPA boom. It featured in many of the beers that set the template for the style, and it remains hugely popular decades later.

How it's used

Centennial is a workhorse for both bitterness and aroma. It's often stacked with Cascade, Simcoe and Chinook in classic West Coast recipes, and increasingly paired with modern tropical hops for a citrus lift. It's rarely the whole story, but it's frequently the reliable backbone.
