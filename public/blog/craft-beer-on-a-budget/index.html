Craft beer on a budget: how to drink well for less

Published 5 July 2026 · by Katie

Craft beer has a reputation for being pricey, and a single fancy can really can cost more than a pint in some pubs. But you don't need deep pockets to drink well, a bit of know-how goes a long way. Here's how to get the most flavour for your money.

The supermarkets are your friend

The best value in craft beer is often on the supermarket shelf, not in a specialist bottle shop. The big four, Tesco, Morrisons, Sainsbury's and Asda, now stock excellent beers from top breweries, frequently on multibuy offers, and their own-brand craft ranges are often brewed by respected breweries for a fraction of the price.

Compare before you buy

The exact same beer can cost noticeably more at one shop than another, and pack sizes muddy the water. This is exactly what MyBeerFinder is built for: search any beer and we show you every supermarket that stocks it and the price at each, so you can spot the cheapest without traipsing around.

Where the value hides

  • Own-brand craft, big flavour, small price, often brewed by names you'd recognise.
  • Multipacks, the per-can price usually drops sharply versus singles.
  • Session beers, lower strength often means lower duty and a lower price, with no loss of flavour.
  • Core-range beers, a brewery's everyday flagship is usually cheaper (and fresher) than a limited release.

Don't sacrifice freshness
