The best beers for beginners: where to start with craft

Published 7 July 2026 · by Katie

Craft beer can feel intimidating from the outside, a wall of weird names, strong percentages and passionate opinions. But getting into it is genuinely fun, and you don't need to know anything to start. Here's a friendly roadmap.

Start soft and fruity

If you're not sure you even like "hoppy" beer, begin with a hazy pale ale or session IPA. These are soft, juicy and low on harsh bitterness, all tropical fruit and citrus. They're the easiest possible introduction to what makes craft beer exciting, and they convert a lot of people who thought they hated IPA.

Or start crisp and clean

Prefer something refreshing and familiar? A good craft lager or pilsner is a brilliant bridge from mass-market beer, same crispness, far more flavour. A wheat beer is another gentle, summery option.

Ease into the dark stuff

Dark beer scares people off, but a milk or oatmeal stout is smooth, sweet and chocolatey, closer to a coffee than a bitter pint. It's a lovely, low-risk way to discover you like dark beer after all.

A few beginner rules

  • Start lower in strength (4–5%) before working up to the 7% flavour bombs.
  • Buy fresh and drink hoppy beers young.
  • Pour it into a glass, you'll taste far more.
  • Keep track of what you like, and chase those flavours.
